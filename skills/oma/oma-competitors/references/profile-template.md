# Profile Template

Save to `research/competitors/<id>/profile.md`. Fill every section, or write `not_found` (checked, nothing there) or `not_checked: <reason>`. Cite a URL after each claim. Use first-party pages for product and pricing facts.

```markdown
# <Name>

- **ID:** <id>  ·  **Tier:** direct | indirect  ·  **Score:** <total>/10
- **Website:** <url>
- **Last checked:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Snapshot
One-line positioning in their own framing (paraphrased), the buyer they target, and their business model. [source]

## Product
- Core job and key capabilities that matter to our buyer. [source]
- Notable limitations or missing pieces, stated by them or in reviews. [source]

## Pricing
| Plan | Price | Billing | Key limits | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
Free tier or trial: yes / no / unknown. Last pricing change seen: date or `not_found`.

## Proof
Customer logos, case studies, review counts and ratings (with platform and date), funding, and user numbers they claim. Label each one as claimed or third-party.

## Strengths vs us
Up to 5 bullets, each tied to evidence and to a dimension of our frame.

## Weaknesses vs us
Up to 5 bullets, each tied to evidence (reviews, missing features, pricing).

## Where we win / where they win
Two short lists the campaign skills can act on. Base them only on facts here and in the brand's knowledge base.
```
