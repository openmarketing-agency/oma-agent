# Landscape Template

Save to `research/competitors.md`. Write it from `index.yaml`, the profiles, the GTM files and the creatives. Every claim links to the competitor file or source behind it. Keep it to what changes campaign choices.

```markdown
# Competitive landscape: <Brand>

- **Last updated:** YYYY-MM-DD  ·  **Candidates scored:** N  ·  **Direct:** N  ·  **Indirect:** N  ·  **References:** N
- **Frame:** <job> for <buyer>, <markets>, price point <price_point>

## Competitor set
| Tier | Competitor | Score | Why they're in this tier | Files |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
(direct first, then indirect, then references. Excluded candidates are counted here and listed in index.yaml.)

## Status quo alternatives
What buyers do instead of buying any product, with evidence.

## Where we win / where we lose
Short lists drawn from the profiles, each with a link.

## Channels: crowded vs open
| Channel | Competitors with high weight | Competitors active | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Angles: crowded vs open
Count of winning posts and ads per angle across direct competitors and references (from each `creatives.md`), compared with `research/own/creatives.md`. Then say which angles are saturated and which are unused but fit our positioning.

## Top adaptable patterns
Up to 5, ranked by strength of evidence × fit with the brand's knowledge base. Each one: pattern, evidence, the channel it fits, what the brand needs. These are the hand-off to `oma-campaigns`.

## Changes since last run
The latest entries from `competitors/changes.md`, or "first run".

## Coverage and gaps
Sources blocked or not checked, and what that means for confidence.
```
