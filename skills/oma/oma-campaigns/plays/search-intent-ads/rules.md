# Search-Intent Ads: rules

## What good looks like
Someone searches "marriage green card lawyer san diego". The ad says exactly that, with the consultation price, and the page it opens says the same thing, shows who will help, and has a booking form above the fold. A week later, the search terms report adds "forms", "free" and "jobs" as negatives, and the ad group with the lowest cost per booked consult gets more of the budget.

## Rhythm
1. Before any spend: tracking confirmed on the booked action.
2. Launch: three ad groups, each with its own page.
3. Daily: spend pacing and disapprovals.
4. Weekly: negatives, pauses, budget moves and one test, all proposed for approval.

## Craft
- One intent per ad group; keywords that mean the same thing go together.
- Exact and phrase match first; broad match only once conversions are flowing.
- The headline repeats the search; the description gives the price or offer and the next step.
- The landing page keeps the ad's promise, shows proof, and asks for one action.
- Negative keywords for research, jobs, free, templates and competitors' brand names unless approved.

## Definition of done
- Ad groups live with matching ads and pages, and tracking firing.
- Every change recorded in the change log with its approval.
- Weekly review sent with spend, cost per lead and proposed changes.

## Non-negotiables
- Spend only from an approved budget, inside daily caps. Every change that spends money is approved first.
- Ads and pages make only claims the brand record supports. Regulated professions follow their advertising rules, such as no guaranteed outcomes for lawyers.
- Check restricted categories before targeting, such as government documents and services.
- Channel rules: `channels/ads.md`, `channels/site.md`.

## Measuring
- Leading: click-through rate, cost per click, landing page conversion rate.
- Lagging: cost per lead and cost per customer against the limit set from customer value.

## Failure modes
- Spending before tracking works.
- Broad match from day one.
- Sending every ad to the homepage.
- Judging an ad group on too few conversions.
