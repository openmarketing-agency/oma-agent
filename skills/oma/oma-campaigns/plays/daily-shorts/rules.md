# Daily Shorts: rules

## What good looks like
One video a day that someone in the audience would send to a friend: a real question answered in under 40 seconds, with the product doing the useful part on screen, captions for silent viewing, and one clear next step.

## Rhythm
1. Weekly: seven ideas, seven scripts, approved as a batch.
2. Daily: one finished video, approved, scheduled and uploaded.
3. Check new comments and questions hourly; review retention by format and actions from the link weekly.

## Formats that work
- Answer to a real comment or search question.
- Before and after, or problem to fix.
- One tip, where the product is the shortcut.
- Myth versus fact, backed by a source.

## Craft
- Hook in the first two seconds: show the result or name the problem.
- One idea per video, 15 to 45 seconds, 9:16.
- Captions on screen; most people watch without sound.
- Show the real product. No stock footage passed off as the product.

## Definition of done
- Script approved, and every claim substantiated.
- Video rendered with captions; audio licensed for commercial use.
- AI and paid-promotion disclosures set where they apply.
- Uploaded with title, description and tracked link; URL recorded.

## Non-negotiables
- No fake users or testimonials; never present actors, staff or AI avatars as customers.
- Health and fitness claims need a source and the user's review before posting.
- Channel rules: `channels/youtube.md`.

## Measuring
- Leading: videos on schedule, average view duration, shares.
- Lagging: link clicks and installs or signups.
- Judge formats after 5 to 8 videos, not one.

## Failure modes
- Long intros before the hook.
- Chasing trends that don't fit the product.
- Changing every variable at once.
