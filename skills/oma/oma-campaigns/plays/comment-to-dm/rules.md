# Comment-to-DM Funnel: rules

## What good looks like
A post teaches something worth saving and ends with "comment ROUTINE and I'll send you the guide". Everyone who comments gets the guide in their DMs within hours, in a friendly message that sounds like the brand. People who reply get real answers and a clear way to buy.

## Rhythm
1. Orchestrator: plans three keyword posts a week, each with one offer.
2. Content-maker: carousel or Reel plus caption. Dm-writer: the message set.
3. After approval, the publisher posts. The hourly job finds eligible new keyword comments; approve and send replies through the publication workflow, not from the scheduled review.
4. The publisher continues conversations inside the messaging window and flags complaints, refunds and medical questions for a person.
5. Check new comments and reply handoffs hourly. Compare comments, replies, clicks and sales per post weekly.

## Craft
- The post must be worth it without commenting. The resource is a bonus, not a hostage.
- One keyword per post, easy to type, shown on the last slide and in the caption.
- The private reply delivers in the first message, with no hoops. Ask a question at the end to start a conversation.
- Follow-ups answer what the person asked. Offer the product once, with a clear link or code.

## Definition of done
- Post published with the keyword in the caption, URL recorded.
- Every keyword commenter received one private reply within the window.
- Conversations needing a person were flagged the same day.

## Non-negotiables
- One private reply per comment, and only within 7 days of it. Follow-ups only after the person replies.
- Never message someone who hasn't commented on or messaged the account.
- Skincare and health claims need substantiation and the user's review before they go out.
- Channel rules: `channels/instagram.md`.

## Measuring
- Leading: keyword comments per post, reply rate to the private reply, clicks.
- Lagging: orders and revenue from DM links or codes.
- Judge a format after five posts.

## Failure modes
- Keyword-bait posts with nothing useful in them.
- Slow replies after the window closes.
- Pushing the product in every message.
