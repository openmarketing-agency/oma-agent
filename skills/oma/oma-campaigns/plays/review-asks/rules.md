# Review Asks and Replies: rules

## What good looks like
Every customer from last week gets the same short, friendly request with a direct link to leave a review, and one reminder. Every review gets a reply within two business days. The profile shows a new post every week. Ratings rise because more ordinary, satisfied customers speak up, not because unhappy ones are filtered out.

## Rhythm
1. Monday: the orchestrator defines last week's cohort; the request-writer prepares the batch; it goes out after approval.
2. Daily: new reviews get reply drafts; negative ones go to the owner too.
3. Weekly: one profile post.
4. Monthly: themes from reviews passed to the owner, such as wait times or billing confusion.

## Craft
- The request is three sentences: thanks, a direct review link, and that honest feedback helps.
- Replies thank the person, mention what they mentioned, and never add details.
- For negative reviews: acknowledge, apologize for the experience, and invite them to call. One reply, never an argument.
- Profile posts show real people and real services, with a booking button.

## Definition of done
- Requests: sent to the whole cohort with the approved template, and logged.
- Replies: approved and posted within two business days, links recorded.
- Posts: published with photo and button, link recorded.

## Non-negotiables
- Ask everyone in the cohort; never filter for happy customers.
- No incentives of any kind for reviews.
- In replies, never confirm that someone is a customer or patient, or mention their visit, treatment or account.
- Channel rules: `channels/business_profile.md`, `channels/email.md`.

## Measuring
- Leading: requests sent, response rate, new reviews.
- Lagging: rating, review count, calls and direction requests.

## Failure modes
- Asking only after good visits, which is review gating.
- Defensive replies to negative reviews.
- Replies that reveal private details.
