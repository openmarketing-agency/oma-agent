# Blog: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-23.

## Who publishes
The publisher uses the authorized CMS integration (for example WordPress or Ghost). For a new article, follow the campaign's publication mode. For authorized changes to an existing article's excerpt, metadata, or other specified fields, update those fields in place and read them back without asking for publication approval. Without the integration, hand the draft or proposed edit to the authorized person and record the verified URL once live.

## Rules
- Every page must be useful to a person on its own. Google treats pages made mainly to rank, at scale, as scaled content abuse, whether a person or AI wrote them.
- Add something real to each page: first-hand experience, product data, screenshots, examples or a named expert.
- Name a real author with a short bio. Never invent authors, credentials or reviews.
- Comparison and alternatives pages are fair: describe competitors accurately, date the comparison, and say where a competitor is the better fit.
- Cite sources for facts and statistics. No invented numbers.
- Don't publish near-duplicate pages that differ only by a swapped keyword or city.

## Limits
- New pages and refreshes stay within the campaign's weekly caps.
- Programmatic pages go out only when each page has unique, useful content.
