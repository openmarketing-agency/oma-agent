# Ads: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-24.

## Who changes what
The publisher, through the ad platform's official API, after approval. Every change that spends money (new campaigns, budget raises, bid strategy changes) needs approval per change. Pausing a losing ad needs none.

## Rules
- Spend only from an approved budget, with daily caps set in the ad account.
- Each ad matches its landing page: same offer, same price, same promise.
- No claims the business can't back up. Regulated businesses follow their profession's advertising rules; for lawyers, no false or misleading statements and no guaranteed outcomes.
- Google Ads restricts ads for government documents and services. Only certified government or authorized providers may run ads for getting visas, immigration status or other official documents; Google adds a "Not a government website" disclosure to Search ads in those categories unless the advertiser is certified as a government provider. A law firm advertises its legal advice and representation, not the documents themselves, and checks the policy before targeting visa searches.
- Respect each platform's special ad categories and targeting limits, such as housing, employment, credit and politics.

## Limits
- Budget changes without approval: 0.
- At most 1 new campaign a week.
