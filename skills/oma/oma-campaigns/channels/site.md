# Site: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-23.

## Who publishes
The publisher uses the authorized CMS or repository integration. A new public page follows the campaign's publication mode. An authorized edit to an existing live page, such as an excerpt, uses the verified target and does not need a separate publication approval. Keep the edit within the assigned scope.

## Rules
- The search rules in `blog.md` apply to every page on the site.
- Landing pages promise only what the product does today. Prices, limits and plans match the live pricing page.
- Forms that collect email say what people will get and how often, and link to the privacy policy.
- Never remove legal pages, cookie consent or accessibility features to improve conversion.

## Limits
- One change at a time on a page being tested, so results can be read.
