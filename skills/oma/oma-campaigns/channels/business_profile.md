# Google Business Profile: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-24.

## Who publishes
The publisher uses the Business Profile API for profile posts and replies to reviews, following the campaign's publication mode. Review requests are sent by email or SMS to customers, never posted on the profile.

## Asking for reviews
- Ask every customer in a defined cohort, for example every patient seen last week. Never ask only happy customers, and never discourage negative reviews.
- No incentives of any kind for reviews: no discounts, gifts or entries.
- Neutral wording. Ask for honest feedback, and never suggest a rating or what to write.
- Staff, owners, friends and family don't review the business.

## Replying to reviews
- Reply to every review, positive or negative, within 2 business days.
- Healthcare and other regulated businesses: never confirm that the reviewer is a patient or client, and never mention treatment, appointments, insurance or any detail the reviewer didn't post. US regulators have fined dental practices for disclosing patient information in review replies. Use a general reply and invite the person to call.
- Never argue, threaten, or pressure anyone to change or remove a review.

## Limits
- 1 request per customer per visit, plus at most 1 reminder.
- Incentives offered: 0.
