# The brand's own Discord server: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-24.

## Who publishes
The publisher, through the bot the brand installed on its own server, after approval of each template or announcement. Answers in a help channel can run on standing approval once the user switches it on. OMA never uses a personal account; self-bots and user-bots are banned.

## Rules
- The bot posts in the server's own channels. No direct messages a member didn't ask for, and no DMs unrelated to the bot's function, such as advertising.
- Don't automate messages just to keep the server looking active.
- The bot says it's a bot, answers from the docs and the brand's own knowledge, links sources, and hands off to a person when it isn't sure.
- Never ask for or accept login credentials.
- Follow the server's own rules and Discord's Community Guidelines. Members under 13 aren't allowed on Discord.

## Limits
- Announcements: at most 1 a day.
- DMs to members who didn't ask: 0.
