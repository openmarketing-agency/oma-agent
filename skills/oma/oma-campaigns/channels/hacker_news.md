# Hacker News: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-23.

## Who posts
A real person, the maker, from their own account. OMA never writes posts or comments for Hacker News, because HN bans generated and AI-edited comments. OMA prepares facts: what it is, how to try it, how it's built, known limits, pricing and likely questions with the facts to answer them. The maker writes everything in their own words.

## Rules
- Show HN only for something people can try now. Not for landing pages, waitlists, fundraising or signup pages. Make it easy to try without signing up where possible.
- Plain title, such as "Show HN: Name - what it does". No hype, no superlatives.
- Never ask anyone to upvote or comment, and never share links that invite voting.
- One Show HN per launch. Never delete and repost.
- The maker answers comments personally, especially critical ones.

## Limits
- No vote requests: 0.
- OMA-written text posted to HN: 0.
