# Instagram: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-24.

## Who publishes
The publisher, through the official Instagram API on a professional (business or creator) account: posts, Reels, carousels, comment replies and private replies. Never through a personal account or by driving the app.

## Messaging rules
- A comment opens one private reply. The business may send a single message to the person who commented, within 7 days of the comment. For Instagram Live, only during the broadcast.
- Follow-ups only after the person replies. Their reply opens the standard 24-hour window, and promotional content is allowed inside it.
- Outside the window, only a human agent may reply manually, within 7 days of the person's last message, using the human agent tag.
- Never message someone who hasn't commented on or messaged the account first.

## Content rules
- Paid partnerships use Instagram's paid partnership label, plus a clear disclosure in the caption.
- Giveaways state the rules, eligibility and how winners are chosen, and say Instagram isn't involved.
- No fake engagement: no bought followers, likes or comments, and no engagement pods.
- Label realistic content made or meaningfully changed with AI where Meta requires it.

## Limits
- At most 2 feed posts a day.
- 1 private reply per comment.
