# YouTube: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-23.

## Who publishes
The publisher, through the official YouTube API, after approval: uploads, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and comment replies.

## Rules
- Disclose realistic content made or meaningfully changed with AI using YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting.
- Turn on the paid promotion disclosure whenever there's a sponsorship or material connection, and say so in the video.
- Each video is made for viewers. Mass-produced or repetitive videos with little variation fall foul of YouTube's spam and inauthentic content policies.
- Use only music, footage and images you have the rights to use commercially.
- Titles and thumbnails match what the video shows.
- Never present actors, AI avatars or staff as real customers.

## Limits
- At most 2 uploads a day.
- Reply to comments; don't post the same reply text on many videos.
