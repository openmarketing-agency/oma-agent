# Email: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-23. Covers newsletters, lifecycle email (welcome, abandoned cart, win-back) and one-to-one outreach.

## Who sends
The publisher uses the authorized sending service or mailbox. New outbound sends follow the campaign's publication mode; preparing a draft or editing an existing authorized sequence without sending or activating it does not need publication approval.

## Consent
- Marketing email goes only to people who agreed to receive it, or where the law allows email without prior consent, such as an existing customer and similar products in the UK and EU, with an easy opt-out in every message. Follow the strictest rule that applies to the recipient.
- Use the consent status recorded in the store or list. Never email people who unsubscribed or never opted in, and never buy or scrape lists.
- One-to-one outreach, such as podcast or press pitches, goes to a real person whose role makes the email relevant, written for them, with a way to say no.

## Every commercial email
- Honest sender name and subject line that matches the content.
- The sender's identity and a valid postal address in the footer.
- A working unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe headers for bulk mail. Honor opt-outs promptly; US law allows at most 10 business days, and Gmail expects bulk senders to act within 2 days.
- Authenticated sending domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).

## Limits
- Keep the spam complaint rate below 0.1%, and never let it reach 0.3%.
- At most 2 marketing sends a week to the same list.
- Abandoned cart: at most 3 emails per cart, and stop as soon as the order is placed.
