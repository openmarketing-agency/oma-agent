# Podcasts: channel rules

Checked 2026-11-24.

## Who does what
OMA finds shows, researches each host and episode, and drafts the pitch and a one-page guest sheet. The pitch goes out by email from the guest's own mailbox, after approval, so the rules in `email.md` apply. The guest records the appearance themselves.

## Rules
- Pitch only shows where the guest has real expertise for that audience. Say why this guest fits this show, with one or two specific episode references.
- Each pitch is written for one host. No mail merges with swapped names.
- Offer topics, not a product pitch. The product comes up only where it's honest and relevant.
- No paying for appearances unless it's a disclosed sponsorship.
- Claims the guest makes on air must be true and their own.

## Limits
- At most 1 follow-up per pitch, at least a week later.
- At most 10 new pitches a week.
