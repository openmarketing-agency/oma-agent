# Summary Template

Save to `<target-root>/creatives.md`. Base it only on the collected records and link to them. Times are in UTC unless the target's market has one clear time zone; say which one you used.

```markdown
# Creatives: <Target name>

- **Last collected:** YYYY-MM-DD  ·  **Posts:** N across N platforms  ·  **Ad concepts:** N across N libraries  ·  **Winners:** N

## Coverage
| Source | Status | Records | Visible | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Copy each source's `coverage.status` and note exactly; `limited` sources list their `visible_count`. Unsupported platforms from `sources.yaml` get a row marked `not_checked`.

## Organic by platform
| Platform | Followers | Posts / week (90d) | Usual posting days and hours | Format mix | Median engagement | Winners |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Paid by library
| Library | Active concepts | Longest running (days) | Format mix | Main angles | Landing offers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Winning patterns
Up to 5. Each one: the pattern (hook type + angle + format + structure), the records showing it, the platform it works on, and its evidence (days running, variants, engagement multiple).

## Caption patterns
Recurring structures (length, opening line type, CTA placement, hashtag use), described in your own words.

## Organic to paid
Posts that were later promoted as ads, and how long the ads ran.

## Notes for strategy
What a campaign agent should take from this: channels and formats to prioritize, angles to adapt, saturated angles to avoid. Facts only, linked to records.
```
