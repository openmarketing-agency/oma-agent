# B2B SaaS playbook template

Use when a software product needs more qualified trials or demos, not simply more traffic. Verify the buyer and user roles, the buying trigger, the actual conversion path, and the available funnel data before adapting this example.

```yaml
id: marketing-strategy
name: B2B SaaS Marketing Strategy
description: Help qualified operations teams discover and adopt the product.
strategy: |-
  What the product is
  Client onboarding software for agencies that need to see who owns each step, what is due, and where a handoff is stalled.

  Positioning
  Make ownership visible without assembling status from spreadsheets and chat. Show a real workflow instead of claiming faster onboarding without evidence.

  Who we market to
  Operations leads choose the workflow and champion the trial. Account managers use it every day and can show whether handoffs are clearer. Agency owners approve paid adoption when the workflow fits their business.

  What success looks like
  Qualified buyers discover the product, put a real onboarding workflow through it, and convert to paid team use if it solves their handoff problem.

  Channels and their roles
  - Website and SEO: answer high-intent workflow questions and make the trial path clear.
  - Docs and lifecycle email: help a trial team complete its first workflow; invest more when setup evidence shows a recurring gap.
  - Founder social and release announcements: carry genuine product or customer proof. Defer broad social and paid acquisition until activation and attribution are usable.

  What we talk about
  Real onboarding workflows, ownership during handoffs, and approved stories about how teams work.

  Voice
  Plain and specific. Show the workflow and who it helps; avoid generic productivity claims.

  Measuring progress
  Track relevant search visits, qualified trials, first completed workflows, and paid conversions separately. Verify event definitions, authorized access, sources, and the baseline before setting targets. If only trials are countable, report activation as unknown.

  Claims
  Use current product evidence and approved customer proof. Do not promise time savings, integrations, or compliance without support.

  Revisit when the target buyer, trial path, or pricing changes. Leave weekly execution decisions to campaigns.
```

For a sales-led product, replace trial activation with a meaningful sales outcome such as qualified demos held, and use authorized CRM evidence when available. Leave campaign execution, staffing, and schedules to `oma-campaigns`.
