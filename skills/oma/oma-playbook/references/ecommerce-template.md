# Ecommerce playbook template

Use when a storefront needs more qualified purchases or repeat orders. Verify the priority products, buyer occasion, stock and shipping constraints, purchase tracking, and any claims or promotions that the brand has approved.

```yaml
id: marketing-strategy
name: Ecommerce Marketing Strategy
description: Grow purchases of the priority collection from buyers it can serve.
strategy: |-
  What the product is
  A collection of desk organizers made for small workspaces.

  Positioning
  Help shoppers choose an organizer that fits without guessing. Lead with verified dimensions, materials, and usable photos rather than general lifestyle claims.

  Who we market to
  Start with shoppers who have measured a small desk and are ready to compare options. Reach existing buyers with suitable complementary products only where repeat purchase evidence and channel permission support it. Focus on regions the store can serve.

  What success looks like
  More shoppers find the right product, complete checkout, and keep what they buy. Repeat purchases matter where the range offers a useful reason to return.

  Channels and their roles
  - Storefront and SEO: serve product and collection queries. Make availability, sizing, and checkout reliable before increasing traffic.
  - Existing social and email: demonstrate product fit or help buyers discover a relevant new collection. Use only channels that reach these shoppers.
  - Collection launches: amplify in-stock releases with approved assets. Defer paid promotion until purchase attribution and inventory coverage are reliable.

  What we talk about
  Real desk setups, size comparisons, product materials, and how pieces fit together.

  Voice
  Practical and specific. Show dimensions and tradeoffs; avoid false urgency and vague quality claims.

  Measuring progress
  Track product discovery, cart and checkout steps, completed purchases, and net revenue after refunds where available. Use checkout starts to diagnose friction, not as sales. Verify event and order definitions, regional scope, and comparable periods.

  Claims
  Use approved product specs, shipping terms, images, and promotions. Don't promise delivery times, savings, or product benefits the brand has not substantiated.

  Revisit when the priority collection, fulfillment regions, or purchase patterns materially change. Leave weekly execution decisions to campaigns.
```

If repeat purchases are the priority, define the cohort, reorder window, and eligible channel before proposing retention work. Keep discounts, budgets, and external actions out of the strategy unless the user authorized them.
